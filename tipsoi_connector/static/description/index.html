Tipsoi Connector

Bring your Tipsoi people, devices and attendance into Odoo as native records. Direct API integration — no middleware to run, nothing extra to keep alive.

About Tipsoi

Tipsoi combines AI-powered biometric attendance devices with a complete employee management platform — hardware and software as one system, rather than a reader bolted onto a spreadsheet. “One system. Total control.”

The platform covers a centralized workforce dashboard, AI face recognition with sub-second identification, shift scheduling and rostering, leave management, field-team GPS tracking, biometric access control, visitor and parking management, team-wide notifications by SMS, email and push, and payroll integration. On the hardware side it runs facial-recognition, fingerprint and RFID devices using infrared and RGB cameras.

It is used from small businesses to multinationals, across manufacturing, healthcare, education, government, retail, financial services, agriculture, construction and hospitality. This module is for organisations already running Tipsoi who also run Odoo.

What it does

Your biometric devices already record who came and went. This connector brings that into Odoo automatically, so attendance, employees and devices live where the rest of your HR data lives — and stop being a monthly spreadsheet export.

Attendance, kept current

Punches arrive on a schedule and become hr.attendance records. Late arrivals, early exits and overtime come across with them.

Employees in step

People are matched on their Tipsoi identifier, so an employee is one employee in both systems. Anyone who doesn't match is surfaced, never silently dropped.

Devices and enrolment

See your readers and their live connection state, allocate or revoke a person on a device, and upload a profile photo for face recognition.

One system of record

Tipsoi comes in two shapes: some customers run the full Tipsoi app, others run the Device Portal alone. You pick which one this connector talks to, and it talks to that one only.

That restraint is deliberate. If two systems both believe they own a person record, employee IDs drift apart and attendance ends up attached to the wrong people. So the connector refuses to be configured against both — and tells you if the mode you chose doesn't match what your Tipsoi project actually has.

Built to be debugged

Every sync is logged. One row per run with the window it asked for and counts of what was fetched, created, skipped and failed. When a number looks wrong, that is the first place to look.

Nothing imports twice. Records are keyed on the identifier Tipsoi gives them, so re-running a window changes nothing.

Failures are honest. A photo with no detectable face is reported as exactly that, on the employee, instead of being retried forever. An inactive Tipsoi account reads as inactive, not as a password problem.

Credentials stay put. Tokens are held on the connection record behind an administrator group and never written to a log.

Before you install

  • An active Tipsoi account, and API credentials for it.
  • For the Tipsoi app, the API user needs administrator rights with employee-module access; a read-only user cannot sync.
  • Odoo 17.0 or 18.0, with Employees and Attendances installed.

Getting started

Install the module, open Tipsoi › Configuration › Backends, choose the mode that matches your Tipsoi setup, enter your credentials and press Test Connection.
